About

A leading Korean semiconductor & components company.

Headquartered in Korea, KRODOSEN Electronics Co., Ltd. was founded with the R&D and manufacturing of automotive connectors. Through continuous expansion we now deliver integrated circuits, discrete devices and sensors, and have become one of Korea's leading professional semiconductor suppliers.

Home/About
Company

From automotive connectors to a diversified components leader.

KRODOSEN was founded with a focus on the R&D and manufacturing of automotive connectors. With high-quality products and excellent customer service, we quickly established a firm foothold in the market.

As market needs and technology evolved, we continuously expanded our product lines — gradually entering integrated-circuit design, discrete-device manufacturing and sensor technology — and have grown into one of Korea's leading professional semiconductor suppliers.

Through rigorous quality control, we ensure the stability and reliability of every product we ship.

History

Milestones

2009

Company founded

Established as a multilayer ceramic capacitor distributor in the Republic of Korea.

2013

First in-house production line

Opened a precision stamping and insert-moulding line for automotive connectors.

2017

Automotive qualification

Achieved IATF 16949 and launched the first AEC-Q200 qualified series.

2021

Sensors and active devices

Extended the portfolio to hall-effect sensors, current sensing and power management ICs.

2026

Global distribution network

A head office in Mapo-gu and a sales office in Guro-gu anchor a global service network for customers worldwide.
