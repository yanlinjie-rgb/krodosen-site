Certifications

Audited quality,
certified worldwide.

Every KRODOSEN manufacturing site is certified against IATF 16949:2016 and the relevant ISO management-system standards. Group-wide we hold international safety approvals from UL, CSA, TÜV Rheinland and VDE — covering the design, production and assembly of connector parts, modules and wiring harnesses.

System Certification

Management systems & safety approvals

Beyond individual plants, KRODOSEN operates a group-wide quality and environmental management system, and its connector ranges carry the international safety marks required by North American and European OEMs.

Combined approvals
