Multilayer Ceramic Capacitors (MLCC)

Class 1 and Class 2 ceramic capacitors from 01005 to 2220, with X5R, X6S, X7R, X7S and C0G dielectrics. Low-ESL designs support high-frequency decoupling, while 1000 V automotive series cover DC-link and snubber duties.

0.5 pF – 100 µF
