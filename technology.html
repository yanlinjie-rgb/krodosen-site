Technology

Manufacturing technology

Industrial connectors and semiconductor chips are built on the same discipline: micrometre-level tolerances, controlled materials and 100 % test. That discipline is what keeps performance identical from the first unit to the millionth.

Home/Technology
Process

From copper strip and wafer to qualified part

Connector and semiconductor lines both run five controlled stages, with inspection between every step.

STEP 01
Terminal stamping

High-conductivity copper alloy — phosphor bronze and beryllium copper — is stamped to ±0.02 mm and selectively plated.

STEP 02
Housing moulding

LCP and PA9T high-temperature engineering plastics are insert-moulded around the terminals to form the sealed body.

STEP 03
Wafer fabrication

Photolithography, ion implantation and thin-film deposition build the die on 6- and 8-inch wafers.

STEP 04
Assembly & test

Wire bonding or flip-chip, moulding, then 100 % electrical and visual inspection before packing.

STEP 05
Qualification

Thermal cycling −55 to +150 °C, 96 h salt spray, mating-cycle life, vibration and IP67 leak test.
